# Code of Conduct — CRM Local

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community:

- **Using welcoming language** — be friendly and patient with others, especially those new to the project or less experienced.
- **Respecting differing viewpoints** — different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives make the project better.
- **Gracefully accepting constructive criticism** — substantive feedback on code or design is an opportunity to learn, not an attack.
- **Focusing on what's best for the community** — thoughtful trade-offs decisions benefit the project overall.
- **Showing empathy toward other community members** — we're all volunteers contributing in our spare time.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment** of any kind, including but not limited to: repeated unwanted comments, personal attacks, doxxing, or stalking.
- **Publishing others' private information** such as physical or electronic addresses, without explicit permission.
- **Unwelcome sexual attention** — flirtatious comments, sexual jokes, graphic material, or unwelcome hitting on/flirting with others.
- **Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments**, and other unprofessional behavior.
- **Public or private discrimination** based on age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- **Encouraging any of the above behaviors**.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:
- Repository source code and documentation
- Issue tracker and pull request discussions
- Chat rooms, mailing lists, and other communication channels
- In-person events related to the project (such as conferences or meetups)

This Code of Conduct also applies to an individual's representation of the project in public spaces, including use of project social media accounts, or via an official email address, or other channels as a project representative.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at the email address listed in the project's `CONTRIBUTING.md` (or the equivalent communication channel). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the situation. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident, and must not disclose private information about the victim without their consent.

The project team has the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. The project team will also issue warnings or bans for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers follow these Enforcement Guidelines in determining actions to take:

1. **Correction**: Private respectful correction for first-time or minor offenses.
2. **Warning**: Written warning with clarification of the expected behavior.
3. **Temporary Ban**: Temporary exclusion from contributing to the project (issues, PRs, discussions).
4. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent exclusion from contributing to the project, for serious or repeated violations.

Project maintainers who fail to follow or to enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent removal of their project responsibilities.
